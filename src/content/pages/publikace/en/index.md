For ten years I worked on how radiation damages silicon detectors, and on developing new detectors for neutron experiments — at Lund University and the European Spallation Source in Sweden, then at the University of Hamburg and CERN in Switzerland. I design, build, and test detectors, from concept through to published results.

## Technical skills

**Simulations & computing:** Geant4, TRIM/SRIM, Python, C++

**Experimental techniques:** CV-IV, TCT, DLTS, TSC, measurements with radioactive sources and at neutron beamlines

**Hardware:** detector design and construction, beam monitors, taking a concept into operation, soldering and lab work

**Systems:** Linux (CentOS, Ubuntu), test-infrastructure administration

**Science communication:** CERN guide (public tours), popular-science talks

## One concrete result

At the European Spallation Source, I designed a vanadium-based neutron beam monitor and took it from concept through calibration and testing to first-author publication in [Physical Review Accelerators and Beams](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevAccelBeams.23.072901).

## Publications

I've published under three different names — V. Maulerova, V. Maulerova-Subert, and now Vendula Šubert. It's the same person and the same work throughout.
