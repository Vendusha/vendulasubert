## Who's the data controller

Vendula Šubert, [kontakt@vendulasubert.cz](mailto:kontakt@vendulasubert.cz)

## Newsletter

When you sign up, I collect just your email, with your consent — nothing else. You confirm the sign-up by clicking a link in the email you get afterwards; without that, the subscription doesn't go through. The email is processed by MailerLite, and I keep it until you unsubscribe. You can unsubscribe anytime via the link at the bottom of every email I send.

## Site traffic

I measure page views with GoatCounter — no cookies, just aggregate, anonymous counts. I do this on the basis of a legitimate interest in having at least some idea whether anyone's actually reading what I write. You can turn off tracking for your browser anytime via the link in the site's footer.

## Embedded videos

Some posts, and the Music page, have embedded YouTube videos (via youtube-nocookie.com, i.e. privacy-enhanced mode). Google may learn that you've visited the page because of this.

## Hosting

The site runs on GitHub Pages, so standard technical logs (like your IP address) are processed by GitHub.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your data. Just email [kontakt@vendulasubert.cz](mailto:kontakt@vendulasubert.cz). If you feel your data is being mishandled, you can file a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů).
