I'm finishing my first comic novel. It's called **ALEFUJ!**, and it's half detective story set among famous Nobel-laureate physicists, half humorous storytelling in the tradition of the Czech comic classic Saturnin, or of Jerome K. Jerome.

**Status:** finishing the manuscript, getting it ready for editing.

More about the book — excerpts, updates, publication details — at [alefuj.cz](https://alefuj.cz).
