I'm finishing my first comic novel. It's called **YOUCKY!**, and it's half detective story set among famous Nobel-laureate physicists, half humorous storytelling in the tradition of the Czech comic classic Saturnin, or of Jerome K. Jerome.

**Status:** now going through editing with [Barbora Miklasová](https://www.linkedin.com/in/barbora-miklasov%C3%A1-42b457a2/).

More about the book — excerpts, updates, publication details — at [alefuj.cz](https://alefuj.cz/en/).
